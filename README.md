## Entry

This entry visualizes the world news of **24 November 2026** across six major stories spanning geopolitics, technology, law, and sports:

- **Trump warns Iran** — threatens "very traumatic" outcome if nuclear deal fails; US considers second carrier group to Middle East
- **Ukraine sinks Russian warship off Libya** — Kyiv claims destruction of a Russian naval vessel in the Mediterranean
- **Nvidia CEO crisis** — Jensen Huang resigns 12 Nov, returns after Trump-backed pressure campaign; company under US regulatory scrutiny
- **Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral at APEC Shenzhen** — three leaders meet on sidelines of APEC summit
- **Spain Supreme Court drops Puigdemont case** — all charges against Catalan separatist leader Carles Puigdemont dismissed
- **Alcaraz wins first ATP Finals title** — defeats Zverev for maiden season-ending championship

The image uses a 2×3 panel grid with a clean dark theme, distinct color accents per story, and simple flat icons. Date "24 NOVEMBER 2026" is prominently displayed as the title.
